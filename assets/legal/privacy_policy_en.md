# Finance Suit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 July 2026

Finance Suit is provided by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how Finance Suit collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use the mobile application.

## Information you provide

- Account information: display name, email address, Supabase user ID, and authentication credentials. Passwords are processed by Supabase Auth and are not available to us in readable form.
- Financial records: account names and types, currencies, opening and calculated balances, transactions, categories, amounts, dates, counterparties, notes, transfers, salary records, debts, and held amounts.
- Work records: work dates, start and end times, breaks, durations, rates, overtime, holidays, adjustments, salary periods, and notes.
- Preferences: language, timezone, currency, week and weekend settings, theme, and report date ranges.
- Communications: information you choose to include when you contact support or submit an account-deletion request.

Do not enter payment-card numbers, bank-login credentials, government identifiers, or other information that Finance Suit does not request.

## Information collected automatically

Supabase and its infrastructure providers process technical information needed to operate and secure the service. This may include IP address, country inferred from IP address, user-agent information, app or operating-system information present in network headers, request timestamps, authentication events, and API or error logs. Finance Suit does not use advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data.

## Information stored only on your device

Theme, selected language, and report-filter preferences may be stored locally using the operating system's application storage. They are not used for advertising. Finance Suit clears these local preferences after a successful in-app account deletion. Uninstalling the app also allows the operating system to remove its local storage.

## How we use information

- Create and secure your account.
- Provide salary, work, account, transaction, held-amount, history, and reporting features.
- Synchronize your records across authenticated sessions.
- Respond to support and deletion requests.
- Detect abuse, investigate failures, and protect users and the service.
- Meet legal obligations and enforce these terms.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use personal data for targeted advertising, ad profiling, credit scoring, lending decisions, employment decisions, or automated financial advice.

## Service providers and disclosures

Finance Suit uses Supabase for authentication, PostgreSQL database hosting, realtime synchronization, API delivery, Edge Functions, security logging, and infrastructure services. Supabase processes data on our behalf under its terms and data-processing commitments. Its infrastructure may use subprocessors in multiple countries.

We may disclose limited information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or during a lawful business reorganization. We do not allow service providers to use Finance Suit user data for their own advertising.

If we later add another provider, such as a transactional email or crash-reporting service, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration before that provider is used in a released build.

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Product tables use Supabase Row Level Security so authenticated users can access only their own records. Privileged credentials are kept in server-side secrets and are not included in the mobile application. No system can guarantee absolute security, so protect your password and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

## Retention and deletion

Active account and product data is retained while your account exists. You may delete it from Settings > Delete account. After password confirmation, Finance Suit hard-deletes the Supabase Auth user and the active profile, preferences, salary, work, account, transaction, category, macro, and held-amount records associated with that user.

Supabase operational logs are retained according to the hosting plan; the current plan exposes logs for up to one day. Provider backup copies, if maintained, may remain in protected disaster-recovery systems for up to seven days before automatic expiry. Deleted data in backups is not restored for ordinary product use. Limited records may be retained longer only when necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Support correspondence may be retained for up to 24 months after the request is resolved unless a longer period is legally required. Anonymous or aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained.

See "How account deletion works" in the app or the public Finance Suit account-deletion page for detailed instructions.

## Your choices and rights

You can review and change your display name, email, password, preferences, and financial records in the app. You may request access, correction, export, restriction, objection, or deletion where applicable law provides those rights. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

## International processing

Supabase and its subprocessors may process information outside your country. Where required, appropriate contractual or legal safeguards are used for international transfers.

## Children

Finance Suit is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when a material change requires it.
