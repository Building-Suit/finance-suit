# Product Spec

Finance Suit is a private personal app for:

- Salary configuration and salary estimates.
- Work days, overtime, extra days, and official holidays worked.
- Account balances, expenses, allowances given, income, freelance income, salary income, and transfers.
- Optional one-level subcategories without changing existing category usage.
- Multiple monthly income sources (salary, allowance received, freelance, or
  custom), each requiring user approval before affecting balances.
- Early or delayed income confirmation using the actual received date and
  amount, followed by automatic percentage splits between active accounts.
- Unified business-date history and reports.

The product is not an employer payroll system, HR system, employee management system, or tax/legal advice product.

Allowance means money given to another person. It is outgoing cash flow, is distinct from expenses in reports, and is never salary income.

An **allowance received** income source is different: it is incoming cash and
uses the regular custom-income transaction direction.
