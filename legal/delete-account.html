Delete a Finance Suit account

Effective 19 July 2026 · Developer: {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. Review the notice, enter your current password, acknowledge the warning, and confirm.

Successful in-app deletion immediately removes the active account and product database records, then removes the authenticated session and Finance Suit preferences from that device.

Request deletion without the app

Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} from the address registered to your Finance Suit account.

Use the subject Finance Suit account deletion. State that you want the account deleted. Never send your password or financial records.

We may verify ownership through the registered email address. After verification, deletion will be completed as soon as reasonably possible and no later than 30 days unless law permits or requires a different period.

Data deleted

  • Supabase authentication account, identities, and active sessions.
  • Profile and application preferences.
  • Salary settings, adjustments, periods, and calculation snapshots.
  • Work entries and official holidays.
  • Finance accounts, derived balances, categories, transactions, transfers, macros, and held amounts.
  • Other active product records linked to the Supabase user ID.

Limited delayed retention

Supabase operational logs are retained according to the hosting plan; the current plan exposes logs for up to one day. Protected backups, if maintained, may retain deleted rows for up to seven days before automatic expiry. Backups are isolated and are not used to restore an individual deleted account.

The minimum information necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or resolving a deletion dispute may be retained. Anonymous information that can no longer reasonably identify you may also be retained.

Important

Deletion cannot be undone. Deleting the mobile app does not delete the server account; use one of the methods above.
